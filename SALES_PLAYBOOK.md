# JobProof Sales Playbook

This is the exact field process for getting the first paid users.

## Positioning

Do not sell "AI content." Sell a finished-job marketing system.

Use this sentence:

> JobProof turns every completed job into a proof page, Google review request, QR code, Google Business Profile post, and social caption so future customers trust you faster.

## Initial niche recommendation

Start with pressure washing.

Why:

- Before/after visuals are obvious.
- Owners already post photos.
- Jobs are frequent.
- Review count matters.
- One job can pay for months of software.

## Daily sales routine

### Morning: prospecting

1. Search Google Maps for `[niche] near [city]`.
2. Open 20 businesses.
3. Add businesses to the in-app Prospect CRM.
4. Score them.
5. Prioritize 7+/10 prospects.

### Afternoon: sample creation

1. Generate a niche demo portfolio in the app.
2. For strong prospects, create a sample matching their service/city.
3. Copy the proof page link.
4. Copy a personalized outreach script from the CRM.

### Evening: outreach

Send 25 messages per day.

Track status:

- Not contacted
- Sample needed
- Sample sent
- Follow up
- Paid
- Lost

## The first paid offer

Do not start with $19/month SaaS if they need help.

Start with:

> I’ll set up 5 recent completed jobs for $49. You get proof pages, QR codes, review request messages, Google posts, and social captions.

Then offer:

> If useful, keep using it for $19/month.

## Objection handling

### “I already post on Facebook.”

Totally. JobProof doesn’t replace that. It turns the same job into more assets: a proof page, review request, Google post, QR code, and portfolio item. Facebook posts disappear quickly; proof pages are reusable when future customers ask for examples.

### “I don’t need another tool.”

That makes sense. The beta package is done-for-you: send photos and notes, and I’ll create the first 5 pages. If it doesn’t save time, don’t continue.

### “Will this get me more reviews?”

It helps you ask consistently and quickly, which is the part most businesses miss. It does not guarantee reviews and it asks for honest feedback only.

### “Can I ask only happy customers?”

No. That can become review gating. JobProof is built around asking real customers for honest feedback without filtering.

### “Why not just use ChatGPT?”

You can, but ChatGPT alone does not give you proof pages, QR codes, a portfolio, lead capture, CSV exports, a repeatable workflow, or customer-ready links.

## Follow-up schedule

- Day 0: send personalized message
- Day 2: follow up with one-sentence reminder
- Day 5: send useful tip and offer free sample
- Day 10: final check-in

## Minimum success metrics

After 100 targeted contacts:

- 10 replies
- 3 free sample requests
- 1 paid beta customer

If worse than this, change niche or improve the sample.

## What to send after payment

Subject: Your JobProof setup

Hi [Name],

Thanks — here’s what I need to build your first 5 JobProof pages:

1. 2-5 photos per job
2. Service type for each job
3. City/neighborhood
4. One sentence about what was done
5. Your Google review link
6. Best phone number / website for quote requests

I’ll send back:

- 5 proof page links
- QR codes
- Google review request messages
- Google Business Profile posts
- Social captions

Turnaround: 24-48 hours.

## When to scale

Only scale after either:

- 3 businesses pay for setup, or
- 10 businesses request samples, or
- one niche gives a much higher reply rate than others.

Until then, keep it manual and close to the customer.
