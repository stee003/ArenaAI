# JobProof Customer Interview Script

Use this before and after demos. The goal is to learn whether the pain is real and whether they will pay.

## Who to interview

Start with owner-operated home-service businesses:

- Pressure washing
- Cleaning
- Landscaping
- Painting
- Handyman
- Mobile detailing
- Junk removal

## Opening message

I'm building a small tool for local service businesses that turns finished jobs into review requests, Google posts, social captions, and project pages. I'm not trying to sell you anything on this call unless it's clearly useful. I mainly want to understand how you currently get reviews and show proof of work.

## Discovery questions

1. How do most new customers find you today?
2. How important are Google reviews for your business?
3. How often do you ask customers for reviews?
4. What do you send customers after a job is complete?
5. Do you post completed jobs to Google Business Profile?
6. Do you post before/after photos anywhere?
7. Do you have a page on your website for recent projects?
8. What stops you from posting every completed job?
9. Have you tried NiceJob, Podium, Jobber marketing tools, or anything similar?
10. What do you pay for marketing or software today?
11. If one extra customer came from this each month, what would that be worth?
12. Would you rather use software yourself or have someone set it up for you?

## Demo script

Show them:

1. Business setup
2. Completed job form
3. Generated proof page
4. Review request SMS
5. Google Business Profile post
6. Social caption
7. QR code
8. Portfolio page
9. Lead capture form

Then ask:

1. Would you use this after finishing a job?
2. What part is most useful?
3. What part is unnecessary?
4. What would need to change before you paid?
5. Would you pay $49 for me to set up 5 recent jobs?
6. Would $19/month make sense after that?

## Close if interested

If this is useful, I can set up your first 5 job proof pages for $49 while I'm testing it. You'll get QR codes, review request copy, Google posts, social captions, and a small portfolio page. If it doesn't feel useful after the first batch, no need to continue.

## Strong buying signals

- They ask if it works for their business now.
- They ask how much it costs.
- They mention specific jobs they could upload.
- They complain about remembering to ask for reviews.
- They compare it to a tool they already pay for.
- They ask if you can do it for them.

## Weak signals

- They say "cool idea" but do not offer real jobs.
- They will only use it if free.
- They have no recent jobs/photos.
- They do not care about reviews or Google.
- They already have an agency doing all of this well.
