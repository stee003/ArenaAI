# JobProof Review Compliance Guide

JobProof must help businesses ask for honest reviews without manipulating customers or platforms.

## Core rule

Ask real customers for honest feedback. Do not filter who gets asked based on whether they are happy.

## Allowed

- Asking a real customer for an honest review
- Sending a direct Google review link
- Using a QR code that points to the review page
- Responding to positive and negative reviews professionally
- Asking every customer with the same neutral language
- Asking for feedback after a completed job

## Not allowed

- Asking only happy customers to review publicly
- Asking unhappy customers to use a private form instead of Google
- Saying "please leave us a 5-star review"
- Offering discounts, gifts, or rewards for reviews
- Writing reviews for customers
- Asking employees/friends/family to fake reviews
- Hiding or discouraging negative reviews

## Safe wording

Use:

> If you have a minute, we’d appreciate your honest feedback about your experience.

Use:

> Your feedback helps local customers understand what it is like to work with us.

Avoid:

> Please leave us a 5-star review.

Avoid:

> If you were happy, leave us a Google review. If not, contact us privately.

## Negative review response template

> Thank you for the feedback. We’re sorry your experience did not meet expectations. Please contact us directly so we can understand what happened and work toward a fair resolution.

## Positive review response template

> Thank you for sharing your honest feedback. We appreciate you choosing us, and we’re glad to hear the work made a difference. It was a pleasure helping with your project.

## Product rule

Do not add a feature that asks customers to rate privately first and only sends satisfied customers to Google. That is review gating.
